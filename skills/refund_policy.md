name: Refund and Chargeback Policy
description: Use when a customer wants money back: a refund request, a double charge, a wrong debit, a chargeback, or a billing dispute about their own account.

Procedure:
1. Ask for the transaction reference and the date, if they have not given it.
2. Check the timing rule:
   Within 30 days - refund to the original payment method.
   Over 30 days - a credit note only.
3. Check the amount rule:
   Up to Rs 20,000 - the support agent can approve it.
   Above Rs 20,000 - it must go to the payments team for approval.
4. Tell the customer the expected time: 5 to 7 working days to the original method.
5. Raise a ticket and give them the reference.

Never promise a refund before the timing and amount rules are checked.
Always end your reply with a line in this form:
DECISION: <approved / needs approval / credit note> | REASON: <one short line>
